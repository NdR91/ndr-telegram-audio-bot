### Task S2.1: adapter specifici e catalogo per ruolo

**Files:** creare `bot/transcription/adapters/{__init__,base,openai,gemini,openrouter}.py`,
`catalog.py`, `tests/v2/test_adapters.py`, `test_catalog.py`; eventuale modifica
motivata di `requirements.txt` e CONTRIBUTING; aggiungere fonti e versioni a
`docs/audits/2026-10-25-v2-provider-contracts.md`.

**Interfaces:** `ServiceAdapter` protocol in `base.py` con metodi async
`list_models(role: ModelRole) -> list[ModelChoice]`,
`audio_to_text(audio: Path, model: str, instructions: str) -> StageResult`,
`transcribe(audio: Path, model: str) -> StageResult`,
`refine(text: str, model: str, system: str, template: str) -> StageResult`,
`aclose() -> None`; metodo sincrono `accepted_formats(model: str, role: ModelRole) -> frozenset[str]`.
`CatalogService(pool: ProviderPool)` espone `async choices(service: ServiceId, credential_version: int, role: ModelRole) -> list[ModelChoice]`.
Per i suoi test, simulare `pool.for_credential`; il pool concreto arriva in S2.2.
